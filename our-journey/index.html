Our Journey

Entry Class 2022 / 2023 - London North Regionals

This was our first real taste of the competition - and we loved every moment of it. Accel was our Entry Class car, and it allowed us to get the Fastest Car award, and Best Engineered car (for Entry Class). Combined with other high-quality deliverables such as an in-depth portfolio, we also won 1st place in Entry Class.

Dev Class 2023 / 2024 - London South Regionals

Coming into the competition with the aim of progressing onto the National Finals, we knew we had to step up our game. Velocity was our Dev Class car for Regionals, and with it we won the Research & Development award for all classes, and again got 1st overall. This meant for the first time, we were going to the UK Nationals Finals!

Dev Class 2023 / 2024 - UK National Finals

The UK Nationals was a whole new level of competition - and for it, Velocity NF was our car. We won the Research & Development award for Dev Class, Fastest Car award for Dev Class, Best Engineered Car award for Dev Class, and got 1st place overall. This meant we were the UK National Champions for Dev Class!

Pro Class 2025 / 26 - London South Regionals

After a year off from the competition to focus on GCSEs, we returned with our Pro Class car - which allowed us to win the Best Engineered Car award for Pro Class, and also get nominated for the Research & Development award for all classes. Combined with the Lightning Reaction Time award, and other high quality deliverables which allowed us to win the Team Identity award, we secured 1st place, continuing our unbeaten legacy, and qualifying us for the UK National Finals once again.
